Data sources and interpretation

The map and component history use saved preparation snapshots. Only components used by the collected circuits are available; this is not a complete chip survey. Dynamic control-flow bodies were not expanded in the saved snapshots. Repeated references to the same value at the same calibration timestamp are counted once. Conflicting values are marked unavailable rather than averaged. Gate estimates, readout errors and coherence times describe different properties; they do not explain an individual histogram outcome on their own. A calibration timestamp identifies the backend snapshot, not necessarily the measurement time of each property.

History shows recorded observations without interpolation or confidence intervals. No per-component calibration uncertainty was saved.
